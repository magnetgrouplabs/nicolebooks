<objective>
Build the deterministic heart of the parse pipeline: the Zod-driven validation gate (money -> integer cents, dates -> ISO, subtotal+tax=total cross-check) and the deterministic-weighted per-field confidence scorer. These pure modules are the authority over model output — a hallucinated total that fails arithmetic gets flagged regardless of what the model self-reports. Satisfies PARSE-04.

Purpose: This is where prompt-injection and number-OCR hallucinations are neutralized: the model's numbers are re-derived and cross-checked locally, never trusted on their face.
Output: src/main/parse/validate.ts and src/main/parse/confidence.ts, both fully unit-tested.
</objective>
